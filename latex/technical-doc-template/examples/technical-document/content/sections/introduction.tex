\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

\lettrine[lines=2, slope=0em, loversize=0.14, nindent=0pt, lhang=0.3, lraise=0]{T}{HE}
sensor channel on this board is sampled by the microcontroller's 12-bit ADC at
\qty{10}{\kilo\hertz}. Any signal or interference above half the sample rate
is not removed by sampling, it is folded back into the wanted band where it
cannot be told apart from the real signal, so it has to be filtered out before
the converter and not afterwards in firmware~\cite{horowitz_hill_2015}.

This note records the design of that filter. It covers the requirements the
filter has to meet in \cref{sec:requirements}, the circuit and the choice of
component values in \cref{sec:design}, the performance predicted from those
values across component tolerance in \cref{sec:performance}, how the firmware
samples the filtered signal in \cref{sec:firmware}, and the tests planned for
the first boards in \cref{sec:verification}.

\subsection{Scope}

The note covers the filter stage only, from the output of the sensor amplifier
to the ADC pin, as shown in \cref{fig:signalChain} on the title page. The
sensor amplifier and the digital processing that follows the converter are
covered in their own documents. All of the performance figures here are
calculated, as no boards have been built at this issue, and
\cref{sec:verification} lists what will be measured to confirm them.
